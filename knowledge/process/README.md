# Drop your newsroom SOPs here

Anything in this folder is treated as ground truth by `/learn-process` and the daily brief.
Markdown or plain text works best. Useful things to add:

- `workflow.md` – how a story goes from idea to published (use the template below)
- `style-guide.md` – house style, headline rules, banned words, how to credit sources
- `beats.md` – who covers what, working hours, how many pieces each writer can take per day
- `wins.md` – links to articles that did really well and why you think they worked
- `no-go.md` – topics/sources Memeburn avoids

## workflow.md template

```
## Ideas
Where do ideas come from today? Who decides? By what time?

## Assignment
How are stories assigned (Slack thread, Trello, editor DMs)? Daily quota per writer?

## Writing
Target length by format. Sourcing rules. SA localisation expectations. Images (where from?).

## Editing & SEO
Who edits? SEO checklist (focus keyword, meta description, internal links)?

## Publishing
CMS steps, categories/tags used, social distribution, when (best hours).

## Measuring
Which numbers matter (pageviews, search clicks, time on page)? Where do they live (GA4, Search Console)?
```
